# Crop Services

## Crop Recommendation
The AI-powered crop recommendation system suggests the best crops to plant based on:
- Soil parameters (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium levels)
- Climate conditions (Temperature, Humidity, Rainfall)
- Soil pH levels
- Seasonal data

Access: Navigate to Crops → Crop Recommendation in the menu

## Crop Identification
Upload images of crops to identify:
- Crop varieties and species
- Growth stages
- Health indicators
Uses AI vision technology for accurate recognition.

Access: Navigate to Crops → Crop Identification

## Crop Rotation Recommendation
Plan optimal crop rotations to:
- Maintain soil fertility
- Prevent soil-borne diseases
- Maximize yield over multiple seasons
- Balance nutrient depletion and replenishment

Access: Navigate to Crops → Crop Rotation

## AI Crop Rotation Plan
Generate comprehensive seasonal rotation plans with:
- Detailed timelines for planting and harvesting
- Crop sequencing recommendations
- Yield optimization strategies

Access: Navigate to Crops → Rotation Recommendation

## Crop Analytics Dashboard
Track and analyze:
- Crop prediction history
- Recommendation statistics and charts
- Performance tracking metrics

Access: Navigate to Crops → Crop Dashboard

## Price Prediction
Real-time market insights featuring:
- Top gainers and losers in commodity prices
- Six-month price forecasts using AI
- Searchable commodity database with 20+ crops
- Sortable tables by name, price, and trend

Access: Navigate to Crops → Price Prediction
