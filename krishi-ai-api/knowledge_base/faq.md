# Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

### What is Krishi-AI?
Krishi-AI is a cutting-edge platform that uses artificial intelligence to improve farming practices. It helps farmers make better decisions by providing data-driven insights, ultimately leading to more efficient and productive agriculture.

### Is Krishi-AI free to use?
Basic features are available after creating a free account. Some advanced features may require premium access.

### How do I create an account?
Go to the Login page in the navigation bar, select "Don't have an account? Sign Up", fill in your name, email, and password.

### Which crops are supported?
Krishi-AI supports a wide range of crops including rice, wheat, maize, sugarcane, cotton, pulses, vegetables, fruits, and many more. The crop recommendation system covers major Indian crops.

### Can I use Krishi-AI on mobile?
Yes, the platform is responsive and works on mobile devices, tablets, and desktop computers.

## Technical Questions

### How accurate are the AI predictions?
The AI models are trained on comprehensive agricultural datasets and achieve high accuracy. However, predictions should be used as guidance alongside local agricultural expertise.

### What data do I need to provide?
For crop recommendations: soil NPK values, pH, temperature, humidity, and rainfall data.
For disease detection: clear images of affected plant parts.

### How is my data protected?
Your data is encrypted and stored securely. We follow standard data protection practices and do not share personal information with third parties.

## Platform Questions

### How does crop recommendation work?
The system analyzes soil parameters (N, P, K, pH), climate conditions (temperature, humidity, rainfall), and uses machine learning to recommend the most suitable crops.

### How do I get a soil test?
You can either visit a soil testing center (find one through Help → Soil Testing Centers) or use Soil Image Analysis for a preliminary assessment.

### Can I detect diseases before symptoms appear?
Early detection is possible through regular monitoring and uploading images of any unusual spots or changes in plant appearance.

### How do I contact support?
You can use the AI chatbot for immediate assistance, visit the discussion forum for community help, or contact through the website's contact page.

## Account & Billing

### How do I reset my password?
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email.

### Can I delete my account?
Yes, contact support or access account settings to request account deletion.

### Are there any usage limits?
Free accounts have reasonable usage limits. Premium accounts offer higher limits and additional features.
