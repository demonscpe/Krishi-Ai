# Nursery Services

## Nursery Hub
Central platform for nursery management connecting farmers with plant nurseries.

## Browse Nurseries
Search and discover nurseries:
- Filter by location and specialty
- View nursery profiles and ratings
- Contact nurseries directly

Access: Navigate to Nursery → Browse Nurseries

## My Orders
Track and manage:
- Current and past orders
- Order status and delivery tracking
- Order history

Access: Navigate to Nursery → My Orders

## Inventory Management
For nursery owners:
- Manage plant stock and availability
- Update pricing and quantities
- Track inventory levels

Access: Navigate to Nursery → Inventory

## Nursery Dashboard
Analytics and insights:
- Sales performance
- Popular plants and trends
- Customer engagement metrics

Access: Navigate to Nursery → Dashboard

## Nursery Profile
Manage your nursery's:
- Business information
- Contact details
- Product listings
- Ratings and reviews

Access: Navigate to Nursery → Profile
