# Krishi-AI Platform Overview

## Mission
Krishi-AI is an innovative platform that leverages artificial intelligence to enhance agricultural practices. It helps farmers make better decisions by providing data-driven insights, ultimately leading to more efficient and productive agriculture.

## Key Features
- AI-powered crop recommendations based on soil parameters, climate conditions, and seasonal data
- Plant disease detection and identification using image recognition
- Soil health analysis and fertilizer recommendations
- Crop rotation planning for maintaining soil fertility
- Real-time market price forecasting
- Nursery management platform
- Water management and irrigation planning
- Task and plant care reminders

## How to Get Started
1. Visit the Krishi-AI website
2. Click "Login" in the navigation bar
3. Select "Don't have an account? Sign Up"
4. Fill in your name, email, and password
5. Explore AI-powered tools and services

## Why Use AI in Agriculture
- Optimizes resource usage (water, fertilizer, pesticides)
- Predicts crop yields with high accuracy
- Reduces waste through precision farming
- Improves overall farming efficiency
- Provides early disease detection
- Enables data-driven decision making

## Platform Access
The platform is accessible via web browser at the Krishi-AI website. All features are available after creating a free account.
