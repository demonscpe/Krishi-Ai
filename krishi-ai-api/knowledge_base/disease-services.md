# Plant Disease Services

## Disease Hub
Central platform for plant disease intelligence with AI-powered tools for identification, detection, treatment, and prevention.

## Plant Identification
Identify plant species from images of:
- Leaves
- Fruits
- Stems
Uses AI vision technology for accurate species recognition.

Access: Navigate to Disease → Plant Identification

## Disease Detection
Detect and classify plant diseases using AI:
- Upload plant images for analysis
- Identifies diseases with confidence scores
- Covers multiple crop types

Access: Navigate to Disease → Disease Detection

## Severity Assessment
Assess disease severity including:
- Affected area percentage
- Severity level classification
- Progression tracking

Access: Navigate to Disease → Severity Assessment

## Treatment Recommendations
Get treatment plans with:
- Chemical control options (pesticides, fungicides)
- Organic and biological control methods
- Application instructions and dosages
- Integrated pest management strategies

Access: Navigate to Disease → Treatment

## Prevention Guide
Learn prevention methods:
- Cultural practices to avoid diseases
- Resistant variety selection
- Optimal planting times
- Crop rotation for disease prevention
- Sanitation and field hygiene

Access: Navigate to Disease → Prevention Guide

## AI Plant Doctor
Chat with an AI assistant about plant health:
- Ask questions about symptoms
- Get real-time advice
- Discuss treatment options
- Powered by advanced AI

Access: Navigate to Disease → AI Plant Doctor

## Disease History Dashboard
View and analyze:
- Past disease detection records
- Treatment history
- Analytics and trends
- Downloadable PDF reports

Access: Navigate to Disease → History or Disease Report

## Specialized Disease Detection Engines
- Sugarcane Disease Recognition - Specialized AI for sugarcane diseases
- Paddy Disease Recognition - Specialized AI for paddy/rice diseases
- Combined Disease Recognition - Covers 14+ plant species and 38+ disease classes
