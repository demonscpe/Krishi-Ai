ABSTRACT
================================================================================
Krishi-AI is a comprehensive Smart Agriculture Ecosystem designed to empower
farmers with Artificial Intelligence (AI) and modern technology. Over 60% of
India's population depends on agriculture, yet farmers lack access to modern
tools for informed decision-making. Problems such as wild animal intrusions,
inefficient water usage, undetected disease outbreaks, and lack of real-time
data on soil health, weather, and market prices severely affect agricultural
productivity.

Krishi-AI addresses these challenges by providing a unified web platform that
combines AI-powered crop recommendation, plant disease detection, soil health
analysis, fertilizer recommendation, crop rotation planning, market price
forecasting, irrigation management, nursery management, and an AI-powered
chatbot assistant.

The system is built using a modern technology stack:
- **Frontend:** React 18, Vite, Tailwind CSS, MUI, Chart.js
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL (Sequelize), Firebase Admin
- **AI Service:** Python FastAPI with scikit-learn, TensorFlow, XGBoost
- **AI Assistants:** Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, OpenAI

The system exposes 40+ REST API endpoints across Crop, Soil, Disease, Price,
Chatbot, Irrigation, Mushroom, and Location services. Multiple machine
learning models (Random Forest, SVC, CNN, XGBoost) are used for predictions,
with fallback rule-based engines and AI insight generation for robustness.

The project demonstrates a complete, production-ready architecture with
graceful degradation, modular design, and a farmer-focused user experience.

----------------------------------------------------------------
10. INTRODUCTION
----------------------------------------------------------------
Krishi-AI is a smart agriculture web application that leverages artificial
intelligence and machine learning to provide data-driven insights to farmers.
The platform bridges the gap between traditional farming and modern
technology by offering a unified digital ecosystem where farmers can:

- Receive AI-powered crop recommendations based on soil parameters, climate,
  and seasonal data.
- Detect plant diseases automatically from uploaded images.
- Analyze soil health and get fertilizer recommendations.
- Plan crop rotation to maintain soil fertility.
- Forecast market prices of agricultural commodities.
- Manage irrigation and water usage intelligently.
- Access nursery management and plant ordering services.
- Chat with an AI assistant for instant agricultural guidance.

The platform is architected as three coordinated layers:
1. A React frontend (web application),
2. A Node.js/Express backend (user, auth, crop history, nursery, rent, shop),
3. A Python FastAPI AI service (krishi-ai-api) exposing 40+ ML endpoints.

----------------------------------------------------------------
11. PROBLEM STATEMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------
The agricultural sector faces several critical challenges that this project
aims to solve:

- Over 60% of India's population depends on agriculture, yet farmers lack
  access to modern tools and real-time advisory.
- Wild animal intrusions destroy crops worth crores every year with no
  automated deterrent.
- Inefficient water usage leads to crop failure and resource wastage.
- Disease outbreaks go undetected until it is too late, causing massive
  yield loss.
- Farmers have no real-time data on soil health, weather, or market prices.
- There is a large knowledge gap between agricultural research and the
  farmer at the field level.

----------------------------------------------------------------
12. EXISTING SYSTEM
----------------------------------------------------------------
Traditional agricultural advice relies on:
- Manual field visits and extension officers.
- Printed government advisories and printed soil test reports.
- Generic fertilizer/harvest calendars that do not account for local soil
  and climate conditions.
- Unstructured market price information.
- Manual detection of plant diseases, which is slow and error-prone.

These approaches are slow, not personalized, not data-driven, and often
unavailable to small and marginal farmers in remote areas.

----------------------------------------------------------------
13. PROPOSED SYSTEM
----------------------------------------------------------------
The proposed Krishi-AI system provides:
- A centralized, web-based platform accessible from any browser.
- AI-powered crop, fertilizer, soil, and disease recommendations.
- Image-based automatic disease detection using CNN models.
- Machine learning-based crop recommendation using Random Forest and SVC.
- Rule-based fallback engines when ML models are unavailable, ensuring the
  system is always functional.
- AI insight generation using Gemini/Groq/OpenRouter for enhanced
  recommendations.
- Market price overview and commodity forecasting.
- Irrigation prediction based on soil, crop, and climate parameters.
- Nursery management with plant search, order, and inventory.
- A RAG-powered chatbot assistant with a curated knowledge base.

----------------------------------------------------------------
18. LITERATURE REVIEW
----------------------------------------------------------------
The integration of Artificial Intelligence in agriculture has been studied
extensively. Key areas of research include:

- **Crop Recommendation:** Machine learning models such as Random Forest,
  Support Vector Machines, and Decision Trees are commonly used to recommend
  suitable crops based on soil nutrient composition (N, P, K), pH, and
  climate parameters.

- **Plant Disease Detection:** Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) have
  proven highly effective for image-based plant disease classification.
  Datasets such as the PlantVillage dataset (87,000+ images) are widely used.

- **Soil Health Analysis:** Soil quality is determined by macronutrients
  (N, P, K), pH, electrical conductivity (EC), and organic carbon (OC).
  ML models and rule-based scoring systems are used to grade soil health.

- **Fertilizer Recommendation:** Rule engines and classifiers map soil
  nutrient values to appropriate fertilizer recommendations.

- **Price Forecasting:** Time-series models and statistical engines forecast
  commodity market prices to help farmers decide when to sell.

- **Chatbots with RAG:** Retrieval-Augmented Generation combines document
  retrieval with LLM generation to provide accurate, context-aware answers.

----------------------------------------------------------------
19. RELATED WORK
----------------------------------------------------------------
Several existing platforms and projects exist in the domain:
- **Plantix** — mobile app for plant disease detection (image-based).
- **Kisan Suvidha** — government app for weather, market prices, and alerts.
- **e-NAM** — National Agriculture Market for online trading.
- **IFFCO Kisan** — mobile app providing crop advisories.
- **Generic ML crop recommendation projects** using scikit-learn.

Krishi-AI differentiates itself by combining all these capabilities into a
single unified, extensible platform with an AI assistant and modular design.

----------------------------------------------------------------
20. RESEARCH GAP
----------------------------------------------------------------
Most existing solutions address a single problem (e.g., disease detection OR
crop recommendation). There is a lack of a unified platform that combines:
- Crop recommendation, crop rotation, and crop identification.
- Disease detection with severity, treatment, prevention, and reporting.
- Soil health analysis with fertilizer and irrigation recommendations.
- Market price forecasting and an AI assistant.
- Nursery management.

Krishi-AI fills this gap by consolidating these features into one modular,
open, and extensible ecosystem.

----------------------------------------------------------------
29. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
----------------------------------------------------------------
Krishi-AI follows a three-tier client-server architecture:

    ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │              FRONTEND (React)                │
    │  React 18 + Vite + Tailwind + MUI + Chart.js │
    └───────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                    │  HTTP / REST (Axios)
    ┌───────────────▼──────────────────────────────┐
    │              BACKEND (Node.js)               │
    │  Express + JWT + Passport + Sequelize        │
    │  Auth | User | Admin | Crop | Nursery |      │
    │  Rent | Shop | Tools                        │
    └───────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                    │  HTTP / REST
    ┌───────────────▼──────────────────────────────┐
    │            AI SERVICE (FastAPI)              │
    │  CropRecommendation | CropRotation | Prices  │
    │  Disease Platform | Soil Platform | Chatbot  │
    │  Irrigation | Mushroom | Location | Paddy    │
    └───────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                    │
    ┌───────────────▼──────────────────────────────┐
    │        DATABASES & EXTERNAL SERVICES         │
    │  PostgreSQL (Sequelize) | Firebase Firestore │
    │  Gemini | Groq | OpenRouter | OpenAI         │
    └──────────────────────────────────────────────┘

----------------------------------------------------------------
30. HIGH-LEVEL DESIGN (HLD)
----------------------------------------------------------------
The platform is split into independent, loosely coupled modules:
1. **Frontend Application** — React SPA with routing.
2. **Backend API Gateway** — Node.js/Express REST API.
3. **AI/ML Service** — FastAPI consolidating all ML endpoints.
4. **Database Layer** — PostgreSQL + Firebase.
5. **AI Assistants** — Gemini/Groq/OpenRouter/OpenAI clients.
6. **Knowledge Base** — RAG documents for the chatbot.

----------------------------------------------------------------
31. LOW-LEVEL DESIGN (LLD)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Each FastAPI module follows a consistent pattern:
  `router.py` (endpoints) -> `schemas.py` (Pydantic) -> `service.py` (logic).
- Each Node.js module follows MVC:
  `*.routes.js` -> `*.controller.js` -> `*.service.js`.
- Models are loaded lazily via `services/ml_models.py` getter functions.
- Fallback engines ensure correctness when models are missing.

----------------------------------------------------------------
32. WORKFLOW DIAGRAM
----------------------------------------------------------------
Disease Detection Pipeline:
    Upload Image -> Validate (type, size) -> Load CNN Model ->
    Preprocess (resize 224/256) -> Predict -> Classify Disease ->
    Return Prediction + Details (symptoms, impact, action)

Crop Recommendation Pipeline:
    Input Soil Params -> Choose ML Model (RF/SVC) ->
    Predict -> Confidence -> Alternatives -> AI Insight (optional) ->
    Return Recommendation

----------------------------------------------------------------
33. USE CASE DIAGRAM
----------------------------------------------------------------
Actors: Farmer, Nursery Owner, Admin, Guest.

Use Cases:
- Farmer: Register, Login, Crop Recommendation, Disease Detection,
  Soil Analysis, Fertilizer Advice, Price Forecast, Chat with Assistant,
  Save Crop History, Manage Profile.
- Nursery Owner: Register Nursery, Manage Plants, Manage Orders,
  View Dashboard.
- Admin: Manage Users, View Analytics, Manage Rentals.
- Guest: Browse, View Market, View News, Contact.

----------------------------------------------------------------
34. ACTIVITY DIAGRAM
----------------------------------------------------------------
Activity: Crop Recommendation
    Start -> Enter Soil/N/Only params -> Validate Input ->
    Load Model -> Fetch AI Insight (optional) ->
    Generate Recommendation -> Display Result -> Chart History -> End.

----------------------------------------------------------------
35. SEQUENCE DIAGRAM
----------------------------------------------------------------
Authentication Flow:
    User -> Frontend -> Backend (POST /api/auth/signup)
    Backend -> DB (create user, hash password)
    Backend -> Email (send verification)
    User -> Backend (POST /api/auth/signin)
    Backend -> JWT (issue token)
    Backend -> Frontend (return token)
    Frontend -> Protected routes (validate token)

----------------------------------------------------------------
36. CLASS DIAGRAM
----------------------------------------------------------------
Core classes:
- User (id, name, email, password_hash, role, phone, location, verified)
- CropRecord (id, user_id, crop_name, season, area, yield, created_at)
- Nursery (id, owner_id, name, location, services)
- Plant (id, nursery_id, name, price, stock, category)
- Order (id, user_id, nursery_id, items, status, total)
- Prediction models (CropPredict, DiseasePredict, SoilHealth, etc.)

----------------------------------------------------------------
37. ER DIAGRAM
----------------------------------------------------------------
Entities: User, CropRecord, Nursery, Plant, Order, DiseaseReport,
Product, RentalEquipment, Task.
Relationships:
- User 1:N CropRecord
- User 1:N DiseaseReport
- User 1:N Order
- Nursery 1:N Plant
- Nursery 1:N Order
- User N:M Product (via marketplace)
- User N:M RentalEquipment (via rental)

----------------------------------------------------------------
38. DATA FLOW DIAGRAM (DFD)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Level 0: User interacts with Frontend -> Backend -> AI Service -> Data.
Level 1: 
- User input -> Crop/Soil/Disease service -> ML model -> Result -> UI.
- User query -> Chatbot -> RAG retrieval -> LLM -> Response -> UI.
- User order -> Nursery service -> DB -> confirmation -> UI.

----------------------------------------------------------------
39. DATABASE DESIGN
----------------------------------------------------------------
The backend uses PostgreSQL (Sequelize ORM) with a graceful fallback. The
nursery module uses Firebase Firestore. Key PostgreSQL tables are defined
in Chapter 7.

----------------------------------------------------------------
40. API DESIGN
----------------------------------------------------------------
The API follows REST conventions. All AI endpoints are under FastAPI
(prefix `/api`). The Node.js backend exposes endpoints under `/api/auth`,
`/api/user`, `/api/admin`, `/api/crop`, `/api/nursery`, `/api/rent`,
`/api/shop`. Full endpoint reference is in API Documentation section.

CHAPTER 8 — AI & MACHINE LEARNING
================================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------
75. AI ARCHITECTURE
----------------------------------------------------------------
Krishi-AI uses a hybrid AI architecture combining:
- Traditional ML models (Random Forest, SVC, Decision Tree, XGBoost) for
  tabular predictions (crop, soil, fertilizer, irrigation, rotation).
- Deep learning CNN models (TensorFlow/Keras) for image classification
  (disease detection, paddy, sugarcane, seed quality).
- LLM-based AI assistants (Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, OpenAI) for natural
  language chat and AI insight generation.
- RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) with a curated knowledge base for
  the chatbot.

Models are loaded lazily via `services/ml_models.py` getter functions.

----------------------------------------------------------------
76. DATASET DESCRIPTION
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Crop recommendation: `Crop_recommendation.csv` (2200 samples).
- Plant disease detection: PlantVillage-style dataset (87,000 images).
- Paddy disease: custom rice disease dataset (15,000 images).
- Soil quality: 3000 samples (N, P, K, pH, EC, OC).
- Fertilizer: 1500 samples.
- Irrigation: simulated irrigation data CSV.
- Mushroom: classic mushroom dataset (22 features).
- Custom datasets generated by `generate_dataset.py` scripts.

----------------------------------------------------------------
77. DATA PREPROCESSING
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Missing value handling and imputation.
- Label encoding for categorical variables (soil type, crop type).
- Feature selection (N, P, K, pH, EC, OC for soil).
- Image resizing (224x224, 256x256) and normalization (/255.0).
- Data augmentation via imbalanced-learn (SMOTE) in training scripts.
- Train/test splits with evaluation metrics.

----------------------------------------------------------------
78. MODEL TRAINING
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Training scripts in each module's `trainer.py` and `dataset/`.
- scikit-learn pipelines for RF/SVC classifiers.
- XGBoost for high-accuracy tabular models.
- TensorFlow/Keras CNN training for image models.
- Models saved as `.pkl` (joblib) and `.h5` (Keras) files in `models/`.

----------------------------------------------------------------
79. MODEL EVALUATION
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score computed per category.
- Crop recommendation accuracy endpoint: `/api/croprecommendation/accuracy`.
- Models report overall + per-category metrics.

Reported metrics (from README):
- Crop Recommendation: 99.1%
- Plant Disease Detection: 96.4%
- Paddy Disease Detection: 94.8%
- Fertilizer Recommendation: 97.2%
- Soil Quality Prediction: 93.5%
- Seed Quality Prediction: 95.1%
- Irrigation Prediction: 94.0%

----------------------------------------------------------------
80. MODEL DEPLOYMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Models stored in `models/` directory.
- Loaded lazily on first use.
- FastAPI serves predictions via REST endpoints.
- Containerized with Docker for production.
- Graceful fallback to rule-based engines when models are unavailable.

124. GLOSSARY
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Crop Rotation: Alternating crops in a field to maintain soil fertility.
- Fertilizer: Substance added to soil to supply nutrients.
- Macronutrients: N, P, K required in large amounts by plants.
- pH: Measure of soil acidity/alkalinity.
- OCR: Optical Character Recognition — extracting text from images.
- RAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation.
- CNN: Convolutional Neural Network.
- YOLO: You Only Look Once — object detection model.
